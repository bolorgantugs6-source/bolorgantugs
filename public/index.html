Language, belief,
and the ways we think.

I'm Bolor Gantugs (Krystal), a student from Ulaanbaatar with a Buryat heritage and an enduring curiosity about how people make meaning: through words, rituals, science, and art.

From founding Mongolia's Brain Bee to studying Latin ritual language, developing translation tools, and designing a symbolic tarot deck, I follow questions across the boundaries of disciplines.

01 / In a few words

Between disciplines,
at home in questions.

My interest in Latin began when I was eight: first through biological taxonomy, then through the mythological worlds behind so many classical names. That curiosity grew into a language association, computational research, and a broader fascination with the places where culture meets cognition.

As someone of Buryat descent, language preservation also feels personal. I'm interested in what happens when knowledge that once traveled by voice or custom finds a new home in technology.

Independent Researcher & Co-Author

Co-authored research on Mongolian sentiment analysis using 2,700+ labeled texts and three machine-learning classifiers (reported 0.90 weighted F1). Also authored computational humanities studies on Latin ritual language and meaning.
